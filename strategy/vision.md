# Vision

**Every Nordic gym runs on Beefcake, and members never think about the software.**

Owner: Malin Ohlsson (CPO). Written 2026-01-14, unchanged since.

## The two halves, and why both are in the sentence

**Every Nordic gym runs on Beefcake** is a market claim. There are roughly 4,000
operators in the four countries. We have 340. The ceiling is not the number of
gyms, it is how many of them can run their whole operation on us rather than on
us plus a spreadsheet plus a separate payment provider plus a door system that
nobody has logged into since it was installed.

**Members never think about the software** is the quality bar. A member should
know the gym's name and not ours. Every time a member notices Beefcake, it is
because something went wrong: a charge failed, a card stopped opening a door, a
class said full when it was not, a receipt arrived in the wrong currency. Our
own product surface for the member is deliberately small and should stay dull.

Put together, the sentence says how we grow: we win operators by being the
system their members never complain about, and we keep them because leaving
means moving 22,000 recurring mandates.

## What the vision rules in

- Everything an operator does daily: members, memberships, money, classes, doors,
  reports.
- Payments as a first-class product, because the alternative is being a thin
  front end on someone else's payment relationship.
- All four Nordic markets in one product, with locale and currency as data.

## What the vision rules out

- Selling to the member. The member is not our customer and never becomes one.
  Consumer fitness features are out for as long as this sentence stands.
- Being a platform other people build on. We serve operators directly.
- Markets where a local payment method we do not support is table stakes, until
  the Nordic four are finished.

## How we would know it is working

- An operator's staff can run a full day without leaving the product.
- Member-originated complaints that reach the operator about *our* behaviour
  trend to zero. Today they arrive through the front desk and we count them
  through support.
- The reason a prospect gives for choosing us is reliability rather than price.

## How we would know it has stopped being true

If we ever ship the feature that makes the member open our app for a reason
other than booking, paying or getting through a door, the second half of the
sentence has quietly been abandoned and we should say so out loud rather than
discover it in a roadmap review.
