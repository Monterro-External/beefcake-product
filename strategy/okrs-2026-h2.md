# OKRs, H2 2026

Period: 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31. Set 2026-06-24, mid-period review 2026-09-30.
Owner: Malin Ohlsson (CPO).

Baselines are measured over the rolling 90 days ending 2026-06-30 and come from
the platform exports in `../signals/platform/` unless another source is named.
Where a baseline was not measurable at the time it was set, the KR says so and
the first job of the quarter is to measure it.

## O1 · Payments people trust

*Pillar 1. Owner: Johan Wirén.*

Members should not hear from us about money more than once a month, and when
they do it should be right.

| # | Key result | Baseline | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KR1.1 | Failed-charge rate across the fleet, as a share of charges attempted in the monthly run | 4.3% | Under 3.0% | `payment-runs.csv` |
| KR1.2 | Members cancelling within 21 days of a dunning sequence being abandoned, as a share of abandoned sequences | 38% | Under 19% | `churn.csv` |
| KR1.3 | Billing-correctness incidents that reach a member's receipt or bank statement | 4 in H1 | 0 | Incident log, support escalations |

Notes. KR1.2 is the involuntary-churn number and the reason pillar 1 exists. It
is deliberately a ratio and not a count, so that a good month for sign-ups does
not flatter it. KR1.3 counts incidents where a member was charged an amount they
could reasonably dispute, whatever the cause.

## O3 · We answer signals faster than they compound

*Cross-pillar. Owner: Malin Ohlsson, operated by the three PMs.*

The cost of a defect is the size of the defect multiplied by how long it stays.
This objective is about the multiplier.

| # | Key result | Baseline | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KR3.1 | Median days from the first support ticket describing a defect to the fix reaching production | 34 days | Under 14 days | Support export, release notes |
| KR3.2 | Share of S-tier tickets carrying a regression clause at handover | Not measured | 100% | `../tickets/`, checked by `validate-tickets` |
| KR3.3 | Signals in the triage backlog older than 30 days | 61 | 0, sustained for the final 6 weeks | Triage queue |

Notes. KR3.1 measures from the *first* ticket, not from the one where we
understood the problem. Most of the 34 days is the gap between the first report
and the point where somebody connected several reports to each other, which is
exactly the part we are trying to shorten. KR3.2 is a process KR and we accepted
one for a reason: the regression clause is what makes a small fix safe, and it
is the first thing dropped when a quarter gets tight.

## What we deliberately did not make a KR

- **Feature delivery counts.** No KR of the form "ship X". Corporate memberships
  is the largest piece of work in the half and it appears here only through
  KR2.1, because shipping it and losing the renewal is a failure.
- **NPS.** It moves too slowly to steer on within a half, and it moves for
  reasons we do not control. We read it as a signal and do not target it.
- **Support ticket volume.** Reducing it is easy and dishonest: a harder-to-reach
  support form would do it. KR3.1 measures the thing we actually want.
