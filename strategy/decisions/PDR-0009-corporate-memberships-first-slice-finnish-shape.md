# Corporate memberships: a first slice, Finnish shape first

Decided by Malin Ohlsson at the prioritisation gate. Door: one-way. Appetite: one quarter of one team. Fund a first slice of corporate memberships: an employer as a payer, an employee list, one invoice, and the Finnish shape (employer pays a share of one membership) built before the Swedish block purchase. The wider initiative keeps its decision date of 2026-12-15. We take our percentage on the whole membership value including the employer's share; the pre-mortem surfaced this as a commercial question with no owner, which is how it would have been answered by whoever wrote the invoicing.

## Rejected alternatives

- Building the Swedish block first, which is what our largest account described and what the pre-mortem's top failure story warns about. The red team's recommendation was adopted: the Finnish shape is harder, it generalises, and the block falls out of it more easily than the reverse.

## Consequences

A quarter of a team, on a one-way door, on the thinnest evidence base of anything currently funded. The red team said so plainly and the argument is recorded rather than resolved. Would change it: SC-1 failing; if an operator cannot set up an employer without us, it does not ship. PDR-0004 said family memberships would be re-examined if corporate memberships was built; that re-examination is now open as BC-0019.
