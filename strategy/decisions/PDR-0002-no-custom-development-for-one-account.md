# No custom development for a single account, including key accounts

Decided by Malin Ohlsson, after a key account asked for per-site invoice numbering. A need becomes a tenant setting available to everyone, or it is declined in writing by the CPO with the reasoning.

## Rejected alternatives

- Building it for the one account: the per-site invoice numbering built for Nordic Fitness Group in 2024 is the precedent, still behind an undocumented flag and implicated in two receipt defects since.
