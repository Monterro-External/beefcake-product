# Vipps before MobilePay, and both behind an evidence bar

Decided by Malin Ohlsson at the gate, with Camilla Roos arguing for both at once. Norway first. Denmark is sequenced behind it and treated as a market entry test with a kill criterion. Vipps starts when Kraft Trening's abandoned sign-up count arrives.

## Rejected alternatives

- Doing both in one piece of work: tempting because the work is adjacent, and it doubles the size of a bet before the first one has returned any evidence.
- Starting Vipps on the strength of "everyone asks for it": everyone asking is a real signal and it does not tell us what it costs us. The abandoned sign-up count does.

## Consequences

Would change it: a Norwegian key account making it a renewal condition, which would move it from an evidence question to a retention question.
