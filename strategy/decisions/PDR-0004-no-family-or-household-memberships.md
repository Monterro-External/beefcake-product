# No family or household memberships

Decided by Malin Ohlsson, after the third quarter in which it was the most frequent feature request in the support queue. No, reviewed annually or when the evidence changes. The volume is high, concentrated in the long tail, with low ARR impact, and it is structurally the same data-model change as corporate memberships without the strategic argument. If corporate memberships is built, this is re-examined, because the second one is cheap if the first exists.

## Rejected alternatives

- Building it because it is asked for most often: frequency of request is not the same as value, and this is the clearest example we have.

## Consequences

Would change it: evidence that it wins or keeps mid-segment operators, rather than that single-site owners ask for it. Nobody has produced that evidence in three years of asking. Re-examination opened as BC-0019 after PDR-0009.
