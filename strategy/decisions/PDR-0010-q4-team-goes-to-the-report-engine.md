# The Q4 team goes to the report engine

Decided by Malin Ohlsson at the prioritisation gate. Door: two-way. Appetite: one team, one quarter. The team that becomes free in Q4 replaces the legacy report engine, held to KR2.3 and engineering-led, with product supplying the observable requirements only. Corporate memberships stays in discovery until its December decision date. Supersedes PDR-0008: the same call, re-decided at the gate with the arithmetic the earlier entry did not have, reach as share of the book and the inversion surviving the removal of the account counted on both sides. This is the record to reopen.

## Rejected alternatives

- Starting corporate memberships now on the strength of the renewal calendar: the shape is unresolved. The Swedish block purchase and the Finnish part-payment are different requests, and a quarter started without that answer is a quarter spent building the Swedish shape and losing the Finnish deals anyway.
- The ranking our own RICE produced on the first pass, 0.56 against 0.40 in corporate memberships' favour, which counted two key accounts as a reach of two. Weighted by share of the book the ranking inverts, and it stays inverted after removing the account that appears on both sides.

## Consequences

If Nordic Fitness Group leaves over corporate memberships, this decision is why. They are simultaneously the renewal this defers and one of the two accounts whose reports do not complete, so the decision is a bet that their real risk is the accumulation of small failures rather than the one missing capability. Would change it: discovery concluding early with one clear shape, the renewal moving, or timings showing the report problem is confined to a single report, which would free the team before the quarter ends.
