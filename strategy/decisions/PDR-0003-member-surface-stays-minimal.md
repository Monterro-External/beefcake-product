# The member surface stays minimal

Decided by Malin Ohlsson, prompted by two proposals to expand the member app. The member app does booking, subscription self-service within what the operator allows, receipts and access. Nothing else without a CPO decision, because the vision says members never think about the software and every feature on the member surface is a reason for them to.

## Rejected alternatives

- The two proposals to expand the member app.

## Consequences

This blocks one branch of the churn tree: letting a member fix a failed payment themselves. That branch may be worth the exception, and it needs to be argued as an exception rather than slipped in.
