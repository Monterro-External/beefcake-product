# Reporting goes ahead of corporate memberships

Superseded by PDR-0010, which re-decides the same call at the gate with the arithmetic behind it. Decided by Malin Ohlsson at the roadmap review, with Elin Sandberg and the three PMs present. The legacy report engine replacement takes the team that becomes free in Q4. Corporate memberships stays in discovery until its December decision date.

## Rejected alternatives

- Starting to build corporate memberships now, on the argument that the renewal is in January: we do not yet know which of the two shapes we are building, and starting without that answer is how a quarter is spent building the Swedish shape and losing the Finnish deals anyway.

## Consequences

If Nordic Fitness Group leaves over corporate memberships, this decision is why. Written down for that reason. Would change it: discovery concluding early with one clear shape, or the renewal moving.
