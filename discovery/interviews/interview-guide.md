# Interview guides

The guide is a reminder of what we are trying to learn. It is never a script. The rule
we hold to: ask about the last time it happened, never about what they would
want. A participant asked what they want will design a product; a participant
asked what they did last Tuesday will describe the problem.

## Round A: freezes, cancellations and the requests behind them

**What we are trying to learn.** How a freeze or a cancellation request actually
travels from a member to the system, what the staff believe the rules are, and
where the workarounds live.

**Warm-up.** How long have you worked here, what does a normal Tuesday look
like, how many people do you speak to in a day.

**The last time.**
1. Tell me about the last member who asked to freeze. What did they say? What
   did you do first?
2. Walk me through it in the system. (Ask them to actually do it, on their own
   screen, with a test member.)
3. What did you tell them about when it starts and when it ends?
4. What do you do when it is not straightforward?
5. Same again for a cancellation. What is different?

**The rules.**
6. What are the rules here for freezing? (Then compare with the tenant's actual
   settings afterwards. The gap is the finding.)
7. Has a member ever come back and said it did not work the way you told them?
8. Who decides when there is an exception?

**Workarounds.** Is there anything you do outside the system? A note, a
spreadsheet, a message to a colleague?

**Close.** If you could change one thing about this part of your job, what would
it be? (Asked last and weighted lightly, because it produces solutions.)

## Round B: what a payment problem feels like

**What we are trying to learn.** What each layer sees when a charge fails, in
what order, and what they believe is happening.

**For members.**
1. Tell me about the last time something happened with your membership payment.
2. What did you receive? Show me, if you still have it.
3. What did you think had happened? What did you do?
4. Did you contact anyone? Who, and what did they say?
5. What would you have wanted to happen instead? (Last.)

**For front desk and site managers.**
1. Walk me through the last time a member came to you about a payment.
2. What can you see about that member's payment? Show me.
3. What do you tell them? Where did you learn to say that?
4. What happens after the 25th? Describe the week.
5. What do you do when you cannot explain it?

**For owners and operations.**
1. How do you find out that a payment run has gone badly?
2. What do you do with that information?
3. What does a member leaving because of a payment problem cost you, and how
   would you know it had happened?
4. What do you tell your board or your bank about churn?

**Recruitment note.** Members are recruited through the operator, which biases
towards members who are still members. Round B added one former member for this
reason and it should have been three.
