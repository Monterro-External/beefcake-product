# Mathias Rud, front desk, Kraft Trening Oslo Storo

Largest of eight sites. Mathias has been at the desk two years and covers the
morning shift, which is when the members who work office hours come in.

## Context

On site, at the desk, between 06:30 and 07:15, which is their busiest hour. We
watched more than we asked.

## What we heard

On the week after the 25th:

> "Monday after the payment run is the worst shift. Everybody who had a problem
> comes in and asks me, and I have the same three sentences for all of them."

**Observed.** Four member conversations in forty minutes, two of them about
money. In both he opened the member profile, looked at the payment list, and
said a version of the same thing: it did not go through, check with your bank,
it will try again.

On the third attempt:

> "I know it tries three times. I do not know when. So I say 'in a few days'."

On what he would want:

> "A date. Just tell me the date it will try again and I will tell them the date.
> That is the whole conversation." [Original: "Bare gi meg datoen."]

On receipts:

> "Members ask for a receipt for their employer. I send it. Sometimes they come
> back and ask me about it and I do not have a good answer, so I send it again."

Asked what they come back about, he could not be specific. "Something about the
numbers." He said it happens perhaps monthly and he has never reported it.

On Vipps:

> "Everyone asks. Every week. I say we do not have it. They look at me like the
> gym is old."

## Surprises

- The next retry date exists in the system and is not shown where the front desk
  looks. His entire request is one field.
- A recurring receipt question he cannot articulate and has never reported. Worth
  following, and not actionable as stated.
- Vipps arrives at the desk weekly at this tenant. It is the third independent
  source in two rounds.

## Implications

- "Give me the date" is the clearest, cheapest and most testable outcome
  statement we have heard in either round. It converts a helpless conversation
  into a specific one.
- Norwegian payment expectations show up at every layer: owner, member and desk.
- Front-desk staff at a key account report almost nothing upward. Their
  escalation path goes through a head office that summarises.

## Follow-ups

- Find out what the front-desk payment view actually shows today and what it
  omits.
- Ask Kraft's head office about the receipt question. Nobody there had heard of
  it either.
