# Hanna, member, Puls Träning Kista

Member for two years, trains three or four times a week, mostly the 18:00
classes. Pays 549 SEK a month by Autogiro. Has our app and uses it to book. She
is the source for most of the member persona.

## Context

Video call in the evening. She had the messages on her phone and read them to us,
which is the only reason we know what the sequence looks like from her side.

## What we heard

On the payment problem, which happened in October:

> "I changed bank. I did not think about the gym at all. Then I got a message
> saying the payment had not gone through."

> "Then another one. And I could not tell if I was supposed to do something, or
> if it would sort itself out, or if I was going to be locked out."

**Observed.** She read the messages aloud. Neither said what she should do, and
neither said what would happen next. She had kept them, which is itself a
finding: she treated them as something that might matter later.

On what she did:

> "I asked at the desk. The boy said it was the system and I should check with my
> bank. I had already changed the bank details at the bank, so I did not know
> what he meant."

On the resolution:

> "It sorted itself out eventually. I do not know how. I assume they took it
> twice the next month, but I did not check."

On the door:

> "That was the part I actually worried about. If it stops working I have to
> stand there and ask, in front of everyone."

On the app:

> "I book classes. That is all I use it for. I would not look in it for anything
> about money, it did not occur to me."

On the waitlist:

> "It is the best thing about it. If I am number two I know I will get in. Number
> six, no chance, but at least I know."

## Surprises

- She kept the failed-payment messages. A member treats these as important and
  we treat them as notifications.
- She never considered looking in the app for payment information, which means
  the member surface is not where this problem gets solved for her.
- The thing she feared most was social embarrassment at the door. The money came
  second.
- She uses the waitlist position as certainty, and her trust in it is high.

## Implications

- The dunning sequence is experienced as one thing and designed as several
  separate messages. It needs an owner and an intended experience.
- Neither message told her what to do or what happens next. That is the whole
  content of the problem for the member layer.
- The front desk's answer was wrong and confident, which matches what we saw in
  round A. The desk cannot see enough to give a better one.
