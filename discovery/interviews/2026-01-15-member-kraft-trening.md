# Ola, member, Kraft Trening Oslo Storo

Member for four years, trains early mornings, does not book classes. Pays 549 NOK
a month by AvtaleGiro. Has the app installed and has opened it twice.

## Context

Short call before his working day. He is a low-engagement member, which is the
majority of the member base and the hardest group to recruit for research.

## What we heard

On payments:

> "It comes out, I do not look. That is what it is supposed to do."

On the one time it did not:

> "Two years ago maybe. The card had expired. I got a message and I fixed it. It
> was fine."

On receipts:

> "I have never looked at a receipt. Why would I?" Then, later: "For work, maybe,
> if I could claim it. I have never tried."

On Vipps, asked directly because Kristin raised it in round A:

> "Everything is Vipps here. Rent, the parking, the kiosk. The gym is one of the
> only things that is not." [Original: "Alt er Vipps her."]

Asked whether it matters:

> "It does not stop me. It is just old-fashioned. My son thinks anything without
> Vipps is a scam." (Laughing, but he repeated it.)

On the door:

> "That has never failed. Good."

On what would make him leave:

> "Price. Or if they closed the six o'clock." Nothing about the software.

## Surprises

- The low-engagement member has no relationship with any of our surfaces and
  that is the correct outcome. He is evidence for the second half of the vision.
- Vipps is generational and reputational rather than functional. He does not
  need it and he thinks less of a business that does not have it.
- Receipts have a latent use he has never tried, which is worth remembering
  before anyone invests in receipt design.

## Implications

- Member research recruited through the operator over-samples the engaged. This
  conversation is a useful correction and we should run three like it.
- The Vipps argument for Norway is not about payment success rates alone. There
  is a credibility component that sales has been describing and we have been
  discounting.

## Follow-ups

- Ask sales whether prospects raise Vipps as capability or as credibility. The
  lost-deal debriefs should say.
