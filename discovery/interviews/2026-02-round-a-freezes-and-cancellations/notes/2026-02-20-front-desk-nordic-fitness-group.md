# Sofia Palm, front desk, Nordic Fitness Group Göteborg Nordstan

One of fifteen sites, one of the largest. Sofia has been at the desk for three
years and trains new desk staff for the region, which makes her the closest thing
to a power user we have met.

## Context

On site during a shift change, which meant two people at the desk and a lot of
handover conversation we were able to listen to.

## What we heard

On the difference at a chain:

> "Members walk in from other sites all the time. Half my job is looking up
> someone whose home gym is Malmö."

On plan changes, which are common here:

> "People move between Flex and Premium constantly. Summer they go down, January
> they go up. I do maybe ten a week."

On what she checks afterwards:

> "I look at the next invoice for anyone I change mid-month. Not always. If it is
> a member who is careful about money, yes."

Asked why:

> "Because it is complicated and I would rather find it than have them find it."

**Observed.** She could not explain what the member would be charged after a
mid-month upgrade, and neither could her colleague. They agreed it "works out
over two months".

On training:

> "I train new people. There is no material. I show them what I do and I tell
> them what to say when it does not work."

On escalation:

> "Head office has a person for it. I send it to her and she sends it to you.
> Sometimes I hear back, usually not."

## Surprises

- The most experienced front-desk person we have met also cannot explain
  proration, and manually checks the result for members she judges likely to
  notice. That is a workaround for something that should be explainable.
- Plan changes at this tenant are a weekly routine rather than an occasional
  event, which makes anything about proration high volume here and rare
  elsewhere.
- Front-desk training material does not exist anywhere in our book of business.
  Every site invents it.

## Implications

- The chain's operational reality differs from the mid segment in kind and not
  only in scale: cross-site lookup and frequent plan changes are daily here.
- Proration is opaque to the people who have to explain it. Whatever else is
  true about it, the explanation is a product gap.
- Escalations from a site pass through head office and lose their detail on the
  way. What we receive is a summary of a summary.
