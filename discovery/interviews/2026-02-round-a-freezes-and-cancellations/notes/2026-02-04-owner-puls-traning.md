# Marcus Sandell, owner, Puls Träning

Four sites around Stockholm, about 2,400 members, fourteen staff. Marcus has run
the business since 2013 and took the third and fourth sites in 2022 and 2024. He
has a part-time bookkeeper and no operations manager.

## Context

Interviewed in the office at Solna, with the reception visible through the glass.
He was interrupted twice by staff, both times about a member's membership rather
than about anything on the floor.

## What we heard

On how he learns something is wrong:

> "I look on Monday morning. If the revenue line looks like last month I close
> the laptop. That is the whole routine, honestly."

On freezes, which was the topic of the round:

> "January is brutal. Everybody signs up and half of them freeze in March. It
> evens out but the month it happens I always think something is broken."

On what he expects from a freeze, asked before we looked at the system:

> "It is a month. You pause a month, you come back, the month is added at the
> end. That is what I tell people and I assume that is what the system does."

We then looked. He was right about the billing and wrong about the binding
period, which extends. He had not known that and thought it was reasonable.

On exceptions:

> "Nadia decides at Kista, and the other three ask me. Which means the rules are
> different at Kista and I know that and it is fine, she is better at it than I
> am."

On reports:

> "I export to Excel. Everything. Your reports are fine but my accountant wants
> the file and I am not going to retype it."

**Observed.** He ran the revenue report, exported it, opened it in Excel and had
built the view he actually wanted in about forty seconds. He has done this many
times. The pivot he built was revenue by site by month, which is not a view we
offer.

**Observed.** When asked to find a specific member he searched for a surname,
got four results, and picked the right one by squinting at the join dates. He
did not use any filter.

## Surprises

- He does not know his churn number and was not embarrassed about it. He knows
  revenue. Churn is something he looks at when revenue has already moved.
- He believes freezes are a fixed number of whole months everywhere. His own
  settings allow more than he thinks.
- He has never logged in as anything other than owner and did not know the site
  filter existed.

## Implications

- The owner persona's actual reporting workflow ends in a spreadsheet. A report
  that cannot be exported is a report that gets rebuilt by hand.
- The gap between what staff believe the freeze rules are and what the tenant's
  settings say is a real risk, and it produces promises to members that the
  system will not keep.
- Revenue is the leading indicator he uses, which means anything that hurts him
  slowly is invisible until it is large.

## Follow-ups

- Compare stated freeze rules with the actual settings at all six round A
  tenants. Doing this at every interview from now on.
- Ask a site manager how the Kista exceptions actually work. Booked for 11-06.
