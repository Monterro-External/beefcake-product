# Interviews

Two research rounds, twelve conversations, and the guides used for each. Notes
are written up within a day of the conversation and are not edited afterwards
except to correct a factual error, which is marked inline.

Round A is written up. Round B was recorded and auto-transcribed and the write-up
never happened: the six transcripts are in `transcripts/`, one per conversation,
each with `written_up: no` in its frontmatter until a note exists next to the
round A ones. A transcript is a recording, not research. Nothing in it counts as
evidence until it has been written up under the rules below.

## How to read these

- **Quotes are verbatim**, translated where the conversation was not in English,
  with the original in brackets where the wording matters.
- **Observed** means we watched it happen. **Reported** means they told us.
  The difference decides how much weight a claim carries.
- Members were recruited through the operator and are identified by first name
  only. Operators reviewed nothing before publication and were told so.
- A persona claim in `../../personas/` must trace to one of these notes.

## What we did not cover, and should

- Round B itself. Six conversations about payment problems, transcribed and never
  written up. Everything the OST and the personas say about payments from the
  member's side rests on what the interviewers remember from the calls.
- Nobody in the Danish market except one former member. Round C should fix that
  before any Denmark decision is made.
- No conversation with an operator who left us. The exit interviews sales runs
  are not research and are not written up in a usable form.
- No instructors, who are the group most affected by class and waitlist
  behaviour and the group we have never spoken to.
