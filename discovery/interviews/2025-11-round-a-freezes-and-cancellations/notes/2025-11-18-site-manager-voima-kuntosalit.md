# Jarno Mäkelä, site manager, Voima Kuntosalit Tampere Keskusta

One of five sites, about 700 members on his floor, nine staff, thirty-two classes
a week. Six years with the company, three as site manager. He is the source for
most of the site-manager persona.

## Context

On site, in the office off the changing rooms. He had the schedule open the whole
time and referred to it constantly.

## What we heard

On his number:

> "Fill rate. That is what I am asked about. Everything else is the regional
> manager's problem." [Original: "Täyttöaste."]

On the waitlist, which took over the interview:

> "I check the list before the popular classes. Eighteen o'clock, always."

Asked why, since promotion is automatic:

> "Because twice it was wrong. Once the class was full on the screen and there
> were places, and once the other way. So now I check. It takes two minutes."

**Observed.** He checked the 18:00 roster by eye against the physical count from
the previous week, which is a habit rather than a calculation. He could not
reproduce either of the two incidents and could not say when they happened.

On what he tells members:

> "I tell them the list works. I check afterwards. If I told them I check they
> would stop trusting it too."

On knowing before members do:

> "The thing I want is twenty minutes. If something is wrong, twenty minutes
> before the members find out, and I can stand at the door and tell them."

On reporting:

> "Everything is for all five sites. My conversation is about this site. I make
> my own numbers on Fridays."

**Observed.** He keeps a spreadsheet with weekly fill rate per class time, typed
in by hand from our schedule screen. Thirty minutes a week, for two years.

## Surprises

- He does not trust the waitlist and has built a manual check on top of it. He
  cannot cite a specific incident, which makes this hard to act on and does not
  make it wrong. Distrust outlives the defect that caused it.
- He hides the manual check from members deliberately, so the distrust is
  invisible to everyone including us.
- Thirty minutes a week of manual data entry to get a per-site view of a number
  we already calculate.

## Implications

- Trust in the waitlist is a measurable thing we have lost somewhere and have
  never measured. Worth finding out whether other site managers do the same
  check, because a habit at one site is an anecdote and a habit at five is a
  finding.
- Per-site views of aggregate reports are a recurring theme across both site
  managers so far.
- "Twenty minutes of warning" is a clear, testable outcome statement for the
  operational surface. Worth carrying into a ticket some day.

## Follow-ups

- Ask every site manager in the round whether they check the roster manually
  before popular classes. Asked at the next one.
- Find out whether the two incidents left any trace we can look at.
