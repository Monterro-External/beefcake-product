# Kristin Aasen, owner, Vestkyst Trening

Three sites, Bergen Sentrum, Åsane and Stavanger. 1,700 members, eleven
employees. Owner since 2018, bought the third site in 2024 and is undecided
about a fourth.

## Context

Video call, forty of the fifty-five minutes with the product open on her screen.
She is the source for most of the owner persona.

## What we heard

On her routine:

> "Monday, ten minutes. Revenue, then how many left. If both look normal I do
> nothing until next Monday." [Original: "Mandag, ti minutter."]

On finding out about problems:

> "The worst thing that happened last year was a member showing me her phone at
> the desk. Something about her payment. I had not seen anything. I did not know
> what to say to her, in my own gym."

> "After that I asked what else I do not know. That is not a good question to
> start asking about a supplier."

On self-serve freeze, which she has switched on:

> "It saves the desk a lot of work. But now I do not know who is frozen unless I
> look, and I do not look."

On the fourth site:

> "The question is whether I can run four with the same office. If I have to hire
> someone to do administration, the fourth site is not worth it."

On Norwegian payments, unprompted:

> "Everyone here pays with Vipps. My members ask why they cannot. I do not have a
> good answer. It is not a reason to leave you, but it is a thing I have to
> explain, and I explain it a lot."

On reports:

> "I cannot see the sites separately without changing something at the top, and I
> can never find it. So I export and do it myself."

**Observed.** The site filter is at the top of the report and she scrolled past
it three times looking for it.

## Surprises

- Enabling self-serve freeze removed work and removed her visibility, and she
  had not connected the two until she said it out loud.
- Vipps came up unprompted in a conversation about freezes. It is a background
  irritation rather than a demand, which is a different and more durable kind of
  signal.
- Her decision about a fourth site is an administration question, which makes
  operational efficiency a growth argument for this segment.

## Implications

- Self-service for members has an owner-visibility cost that nobody designed for.
- The owner's Monday routine is the whole surface. Anything she should know has
  to be there or it does not exist.
- Norway is shallow in a way customers experience monthly and never escalate.

## Follow-ups

- Ask the two other Norwegian tenants about Vipps without prompting. Round B.
- Look at what happens to owner visibility when self-serve freeze is on. Which
  screen would have told her?
