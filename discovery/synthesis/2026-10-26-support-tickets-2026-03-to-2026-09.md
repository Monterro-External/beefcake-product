## Freeze ends on the wrong date

42 tickets, ten tenants, every month: the largest thing in the queue. A freeze
starting on the last day of a month ends early and the membership restarts.
Sub-clusters: wrong end date reported, 26; the member was charged or lost paid
days, 16.

> "Third one this week. Member freezes at the end of a month, comes back a day or
> two before they should, gets charged. I am now checking every freeze by hand
> before I confirm it, which rather defeats the purpose." SUP-10004, front desk, mid.

> "Johan Håkansson has a freeze from 28 February, one month. The membership became
> active again on 28 March. Surely it should be the first?" ("Johan Håkansson har
> uppehåll från 28 februari, en månad. Medlemskapet blev aktivt igen den 28 mars.
> Det ska väl vara den första?") SUP-10010, owner, mid.

**Absent:** kraft-trening and nordic-fitness-group, both key accounts, 29 and 34
tickets in the queue and none here. Every long tail and mid tenant is present,
so no control exists among the segments that file; whether the chains handle
freezes centrally is the first question.
**Stopped or continuing:** continuing, 28 March to 2 September. No release
claims a fix; 14 May named freeze confirmation screens as coming next.
**For triage:** defect, 42 tickets, SUP-10001 to SUP-10383. Desks already correct
by hand ("We correct it manually" ("Vi rättar manuellt"), SUP-10307), so volume understates frequency.
