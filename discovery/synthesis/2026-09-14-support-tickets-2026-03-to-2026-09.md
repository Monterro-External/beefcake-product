## Freeze ends on the wrong date

42 tickets, ten tenants, every month, the largest thing in the queue. A freeze
starting on the last day of a month ends a day early, the membership restarts and
the member is charged; the desk hears it from the member. Sub-clusters: wrong date
alone, 29; a charge to the member named in the ticket, 13.

> "Johan Håkansson has a freeze from 28 February, one month. The membership became
> active again on 28 March. Surely it should be the 1st?" ("Johan Håkansson har
> uppehåll från 28 februari, en månad. Medlemskapet blev aktivt igen den 28 mars.
> Det ska väl vara den första?") SUP-10038, owner, mid.

> "Third one this week. [...] I am now checking every freeze by hand before I
> confirm it, which rather defeats the purpose." SUP-10276, front desk, long tail.

**Absent:** nordic-fitness-group and kraft-trening, both key accounts, zero
against 34 and 29 tickets in the queue. Every long tail and mid tenant is present,
so there is no control among the segments that file freely; whether those two
handle freezes centrally is the first question to ask them.
**Stopped or continuing:** continuing, 2026-03-28 to 2026-09-02, no release
mentions freeze dates.
**For triage:** defect, 42 tickets, SUP-10025 onward. Support answers all 42 by
telling the operator to correct it by hand.

## Payment retried every day

20 tickets, nine tenants. Operators are not asking why a charge failed; they ask
why it retries daily and how to stop it. Fifteen arrive after the 14 May release
that added the next attempt date and a written reason: explaining the retry has
not reduced the retrying.

> "A member is being re-charged daily. Her bank has now blocked us as a recurring
> merchant because of the number of attempts, which means she cannot pay even if
> she wants to." SUP-10284, site manager, mid.

> "Several members are getting payment reminders every day. One asked whether we
> had been hacked." ("Flera medlemmar får betalningspåminnelser varje dag. En
> frågade om vi blivit hackade.") SUP-10186, owner, long tail.

**Absent:** nordic-fitness-group (key account), voima-kuntosalit and aktiv-halsa
(mid). Voima Kuntosalit is the control: 28 tickets, present in other payment
themes, nothing here.
**Stopped or continuing:** continuing past both releases. Five tickets follow the
9 July dunning preview, which that release says does not cover charges already
part way through.
**For triage:** defect, 20 tickets. One member's bank blocked us as a merchant,
which removes her ability to pay at all.
