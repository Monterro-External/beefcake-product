## Theme 1: failed-payment messages and retries every day

Between 3 and 10 August, four accounts report members receiving a failed-payment message every day since the run, in two cases with a charge attempt each time, in one after the member paid at the desk. One member's bank blocked the operator as a recurring merchant. No agent reply says what the retry schedule is.

> "One of them asked whether we had been hacked." SUP-10213, front desk, key account (same sentence in SUP-10214, owner, long tail)

> "She has paid at the desk and they keep coming." [Hon har betalat i receptionen och de fortsätter komma.] SUP-10204, owner, long tail

Sub-clusters: messages continue after the member paid at the desk (1); repeated charge attempts, not only messages (2: SUP-10205, SUP-10216); messages only, no statement on charges (2).
