# Interview synthesis, round A: freezes and cancellations

Round A asked how a freeze or cancellation request travels from member to system,
what staff believe the rules are, and where the workarounds live. Six people at
five tenants: two owners, two site managers, two front desk; four in Sweden, one
in Norway, one in Finland; four mid, one long tail, one key account. All are current customers, two from
one tenant; the READMEs do not say how they were recruited. No recordings
survive: every quote rests on handwritten notes.

## Per-site numbers rebuilt by hand

Three people at three tenants leave the product to get one site's number, and we
watched each workaround. Earlier support and sales reports carry Excel export and
cross-site reporting; new here is that both owners miss a site filter we ship.

> "I cannot see the sites separately without changing something at the top, and I can never find it. So I export and do it myself." [Kristin Aasen, owner](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-15-owner-vestkyst-trening.md>)

> "Everything is for all five sites. My conversation is about this site. I make my own numbers on Fridays." [Jarno Mäkelä, site manager](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-18-site-manager-voima-kuntosalit.md>)

Observed: Marcus Sandell's forty-second revenue-by-site-by-month pivot ([note](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-04-owner-puls-traning.md>)), Kristin scrolling past the filter three times, Jarno's hand-typed fill-rate sheet.

**Worth testing:** why does an owner who needs one site's view not find the control that gives it?

## Staff tell members rules the system does not follow

The person at the counter states the rule with confidence and the system does
something else. An owner had the binding period wrong; a front-desk employee gave
a freezing member a wrong stop date without checking. Both observed. The Q4 2025
ticket theme saw staff asking us to confirm rules; these notes show staff who never ask.

> "It is a month. You pause a month, you come back, the month is added at the end. That is what I tell people and I assume that is what the system does." [Marcus Sandell, owner](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-04-owner-puls-traning.md>)

> "She asked when the money stops and I said next month, which I think is right." [Elias Berg, front desk](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-11-front-desk-gymmet-vasastan.md>)

> "I explain it about twice a week." [Nadia Ohlin, site manager, on notice](<../interviews/2025-12-round-a-freezes-and-cancellations/notes/2025-12-08-site-manager-puls-traning.md>) (reported)

**Worth testing:** at the moment of the transaction, what does the staff member need to know to say the true dates out loud?

## Demoted for length

- **Nobody can see who is frozen or coming back.** New against every earlier report. Nadia keeps eleven returning members on paper (observed): "If I lose these notes I lose those members." Kristin, after self-serve freeze: "now I do not know who is frozen unless I look, and I do not look." Worth testing: does contact before a freeze ends change whether the member returns?
- **A failed payment reaches someone who cannot explain it.** Unprompted. Elias (capability): "I can see it says failed. I cannot see why." Kristin (credibility): "That is not a good question to start asking about a supplier." Elias nearly repeats SUP-09442 (Q4 2025); the 14 May 2026 written reason may have fixed his half, needs checking. Worth testing: how does an owner learn of a member's payment problem first?
- **The desk learns from the desk.** Elias was trained by a colleague and keeps a colleague's note of refusals (observed); Sofia Palm: "There is no material." Worth testing: where would a new desk employee look first?
- **Owners look at revenue on Monday.** Kristin: "Monday, ten minutes." Marcus: "If the revenue line looks like last month I close the laptop." Worth testing: what reaches an owner whose routine is ten minutes?
- **Exceptions decided at the site, unwritten.** Marcus: "Nadia decides at Kista"; Nadia: "nobody has written that down". One tenant, two roles.

- Marcus picked a member from four surname matches by join date (observed, no quote).
