## Freeze from month end returns early

A one-month freeze from a month's last day ends on the 30th instead of the 1st. The member returns early and, in 7 of 18 tickets, is charged a full month. All filed 1 to 5 June, most for freezes from 30 April, in all four languages.

> "I am now checking every freeze by hand before I confirm it, which rather defeats the purpose." SUP-10052, owner, long tail

> "She feels she has been cheated of a month and I cannot explain it." [Hon tycker att hon blivit lurad på en månad och jag kan inte förklara det.] SUP-10034, front desk, long tail

Sub-clusters: charged 7; working around by hand 3 (SUP-10047, 10052, 10054); date only 8. SUP-10031, 10041 and 10057 were answered as if a bank had declined a charge.

**Absent:** traningsverket-lund (long tail, 7 June tickets) is the control: same segment, no freeze ticket; whether it had month-end freezes is the first question. Also absent: nordic-fitness-group, kraft-trening (key), puls-traning (mid). Key accounts are 17% of June and 0% here; the README says they work around rather than file, so this could be fixed by hand at scale there.
**Stopped or continuing:** no release note says it was fixed; the 14 May note's coming freeze screens address the Q1 rules theme, not this defect.
**For triage:** defect, SUP-10029, 10031, 10034, 10036, 10038, 10039, 10041, 10042, 10043, 10045, 10047, 10048, 10050, 10051, 10052, 10053, 10054, 10057. Members are charged for days they froze, a billing-correctness item under O1.
