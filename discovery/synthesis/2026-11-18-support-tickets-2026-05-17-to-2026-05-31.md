## 1. Freeze starting at month end ends early and charges

A freeze that starts on the last day of a month gets an end date one or two days early, and the member is charged on return. Three accounts in three countries describe the same trigger independently, and two of them already correct or check freezes by hand. Mid is 2 of 4 tickets against 3 of 28 in the queue: the one segment that rarely writes in this fortnight writes here.

> "I am now checking every freeze by hand before I confirm it, which rather defeats the purpose." SUP-10028, front desk, mid

> "The system restarted it on 30 May and charged a full month. She thinks she lost a month, and I cannot explain why." (Systemet startet det igjen 30 mai og trakk full måned. Hun mener hun mistet en måned, og jeg klarer ikke å forklare hvorfor.) SUP-10026, site manager, mid

> "The end date on a freeze comes out wrong when it starts on the last day of the month. Has happened twice now." (Slutdatumet på uppehåll blir fel när man börjar sista dagen i månaden. Har hänt två gånger nu.) SUP-10025, owner, long tail

Mis-tagged: 1 of 4 (SUP-10027, tagged `billing, payment`).

**Absent:** Trening Grünerløkka, Fitness Nørrebro, Gymmet Vasastan, Bodyfokus Umeå (long tail); Voima Kuntosalit (mid); Nordic Fitness Group (key account). CONTEXT.md does not say which segments raise problems through support; the signals README says key accounts use workarounds instead. Control: Trening Grünerløkka, 4 other tickets and none here. First question: did it start any freeze on a month's last day in May.
**Stopped or continuing:** no release note claims a fix. The 14 May note lists freeze and notice confirmation screens as "coming next", which is about wording, not end dates.
**For triage:** defect, SUP-10025, SUP-10026, SUP-10027, SUP-10028. The member is charged for days they froze, so this sits under "the member's money is sacred", and SUP-10028 says "Third one this week" from one site: the ticket count understates the incidents.
