### Employer-paid memberships: two shapes, one shared requirement

One lost deal per shape, as H2 2025 predicted. New: both shapes need the same thing when an employee leaves, and the Swedish buyer ranks that above the invoice.

> "The employer tells me now, on a spreadsheet, once a quarter, and we are always three months behind. That is the actual problem, not the invoice." (stockholm-strength-collective-demo.md 15:28, finance, key)

> "We need to be able to take somebody off the employer's list without ending their membership." (kuntokeskus-espoo-demo.md 25:58, operations manager, mid)

**Cost:** SEK 780,000 (Stockholm Strength Collective, to Boxmaster) and EUR 39,000 (Kuntokeskus Espoo, to Boxmaster); debriefs agree with the stated reasons.
**Market or book:** both. Nordic Fitness Group calls it "the renewal item"; Voima turned away two employers (21:09).
**For triage:** deciding; files above plus the two NFG renewal calls. PDR-0009 funds the Finnish shape first and PDR-0010 holds corporate in discovery to 2026-12-15; neither record names leaving the employer, which every buyer named as their minimum.

### Vipps at the desk

A capability need where members sign up at the desk. Two other Norwegian prospects said it does not decide for them: older members, or everyone already on AvtaleGiro.

> "Så vi ber en tjueåring om å logge inn i nettbanken sin mens det står folk bak ham i køen." (So we ask a twenty-year-old to log into their bank while people queue behind him.) (bergen-styrke-demo.md 02:41, owner, mid)

> "Vipps er greit å ha, men det er ikke der jeg taper medlemmer." (Vipps is nice to have, but it is not where I lose members.) (trondheim-trening-demo.md 24:03, owner, mid)

**Cost:** NOK 288,000 (Bergen Styrke, to Gymly). The rep suspects the first payment at sign-up, sharper than the stated reason.
**Market or book:** both; Kraft Trening offered to count sign-ups started and not completed (03:26).
**For triage:** deciding; bergen-styrke, kraft-trening, stavanger-helse, trondheim-trening. PDR-0006 waits on that Kraft count; the calls say the cost is concentrated where members are young.
