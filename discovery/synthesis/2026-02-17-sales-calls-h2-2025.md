# Sales call synthesis, H2 2025

22 recorded calls (14 prospect demos, 5 renewals, 3 expansion) and 9 lost-deal
debriefs. Sales calls are the only source that hears from the market rather than
from our own book, so a theme that appears here and nowhere else is early
information rather than noise.

## What decides deals

Ranked by how often the theme appeared in the last third of a call, which is
where the deciding question usually gets asked.

| # | Theme | Calls | Lost deals | Market |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What happens to our existing payment mandates | 19 | 0 | All |
| 2 | Payment method the market expects | 11 | 4 | NO, DK |
| 3 | Reporting across sites | 9 | 1 | All, mid and up |
| 4 | Corporate or employer-paid memberships | 7 | 2 | SE, FI |
| 5 | Price against Gymly | 6 | 2 | SE, single site |
| 6 | Deployment and data residency | 2 | 1 | SE public sector |

### 1. Mandates decide more deals than features

Nineteen of 22 calls contain a version of the question "what happens to our
existing direct debits". Nobody has ever lost us a deal on it, and it is the
question that most often ends the meeting on a good note when answered well.

> "Send that. That is worth more to me than half the features." Malmö Motion,
> owner, on the migration procedure.

We do this well and we do not talk about it anywhere except in the room.
**Recommendation:** a written migration procedure sales can send, which exists
today only as a document Erik wrote himself.

### 2. Payment method by market

Vipps in Norway appears in every Norwegian call. It cost us two deals outright
in the half. The interesting detail is that it is described in two different
ways: as a capability ("we cannot sign someone up at the desk without it") and as
credibility ("it makes us look old"). The first is operational and measurable.
The second is not, and both were mentioned by the same prospects.

MobilePay in Denmark appears in both Danish calls, both times as question one.

**Recommendation:** treat Norway and Denmark as different problems. Norway is a
missing method in a market where we already have revenue. Denmark is a market
entry question with a second requirement attached, the invoice layout.

### 3. Corporate and employer-paid memberships

Seven calls, two lost deals, and two distinctly different shapes:

- **Sweden:** an employer buys a block of memberships and wants one invoice.
- **Finland:** an employer pays part of one membership as a taxable benefit, the
  member pays the rest.

We have been treating these as the same request. They are not, and a solution for
the Swedish shape would not have won the Finnish deal.

**Recommendation:** if this is picked up, the discovery has to cover both shapes
before anything is scoped.

## What sales asks for that we should not build

- **On-premise deployment.** Two calls, one lost deal, all public sector or
  part-public. Stated non-goal.
- **Price matching against Gymly for single-site gyms.** Six calls. These
  prospects are outside the ICP and the losses are correct losses.

## Caveats

- Recorded calls are a biased sample: reps record the ones that go well.
- Debriefs are written by the person who lost the deal, within a week, and they
  systematically over-weight the reason the customer said and under-weight the
  reason the rep suspects.
- Nothing here tells us about renewal risk in the existing book. Renewal calls
  in this sample are all with accounts that renewed.
