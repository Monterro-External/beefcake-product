## Vipps

The only deal in the period lost on something we lack. Bergen Styrke said it outright and decided on it; Trondheim Trening asked in the last third but said it is not where she loses members. Two shapes under one name: for Bergen it is a capability at the desk (sign a person up in two minutes, first payment taken on the spot), for Trondheim it is an expectation with no cost attached.

> "We cannot sell a membership where people cannot pay with Vipps." ("Vi kan ikke selge et medlemskap hvor folk ikke kan betale med Vipps.") bergen-styrke-demo.md 29:24, owner, Mid

> "So we ask a twenty-year-old to log into his online bank while people queue behind him." ("Så vi ber en tjueåring om å logge inn i nettbanken sin mens det står folk bak ham i køen.") bergen-styrke-demo.md 02:41, owner, Mid

> "Vipps is nice to have, but that is not where I lose members." ("Vipps er greit å ha, men det er ikke der jeg taper medlemmer.") trondheim-trening-demo.md 24:03, owner, Mid

**Cost:** NOK 288,000, Bergen, lost to Gymly. The debrief's stated reason (Vipps) and the rep's suspected reason (Gymly took the first payment immediately and set up the mandate afterwards) agree on the theme but not the shape: the rep thinks the first-payment moment mattered more.

**Market or book:** market, Norway, Mid segment.

**For triage:** deciding; bergen-styrke-demo.md, lost-deals/bergen-styrke.md, trondheim-trening-demo.md. PDR-0006 (accepted 2026-07-28) already sequences this behind Kraft Trening's abandoned sign-up count; what the counts do not show is that the losing moment Bergen described is the first payment at the desk, which is the thing that count should measure. The rep's debrief also asks for a yes or no so he can decide whether to keep working four-site Norwegian gyms.

## Know who has stopped paying without going looking

Two owners, one in Sweden and one in Norway, describe the same job: finding the members who stopped paying before it is too late to act. Karlstad saw it answered in the payment run and praised it twice; Trondheim heard the same answer and rejected it because she has to open the report herself.

> "So I can see who has not paid without opening the bank." ("Så jag ser vilka som inte betalat utan att öppna banken.") gym-karlstad-demo.md 11:20, owner, Long tail

> "The ones who stop paying without cancelling, they are the ones who annoy me most." ("de som slutter å betale uten å si opp, det er de som irriterer meg mest") trondheim-trening-demo.md 24:21, owner, Mid

> "Then I will not open it." ("Da åpner jeg den ikke.") trondheim-trening-demo.md 25:36, owner, Mid

**Cost:** no deal decided on it. Karlstad touches it and shows `same deal`. Trondheim is still open.

**Market or book:** market, SE and NO.

**For triage:** ask and praise; trondheim-trening-demo.md, gym-karlstad-demo.md. The same moment splits by shape: for a single-site owner seeing it in the product is enough, for the ICP owner it fails unless it reaches her unasked. The weekly email is Trondheim's own proposal. This is the involuntary churn O1 targets, heard from a prospect, not the book.
