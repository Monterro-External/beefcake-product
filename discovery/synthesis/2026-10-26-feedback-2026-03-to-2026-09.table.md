# Feedback 2026-03 to 2026-09, one row per item with words

Built for `2026-10-26-feedback-2026-03-to-2026-09.md`. One row per NPS comment,
app review and forwarded member-feedback item. Scored NPS responses without a
comment are not rows; they are in the score tables at the end.

**Voice rule.** NPS: by the respondent's role; every respondent is an owner, site
manager, finance or operations role, so all are `account`. Reviews and member
feedback: by channel, both carry the member's words, so `end user`. Member
feedback is second hand, written by desk staff about a member.

**Account.** NPS and member feedback name the tenant. Reviews: the tenant from
`gym_mentioned` where the review names a gym; otherwise `tenant_guess`, marked
`(guess)`. Review country is the store's `country` column; it does not always
match the language of the text.

**Intent** is written from the words, never from the review title. **Kind** is
`defect`, `request`, `praise`, `did-nothing` or `other`. **Theme** is the cluster
slug from the report; `praise: …` is a praise cluster; `did-nothing` rows are
never a theme; `One account: …` is a single-voice cluster.
