## Daily payment reminders after paying

One failed charge starts a daily message that continues after the member has paid, often at the desk. Members read it as debt collection.

> My card failed once and Nordic Fitness Group has sent me a message every single day since. I have paid. (REV-2008, end user, 2 star, key account)

> Members get chased for payments over and over. It makes us look like a debt collector and it is not our doing. (NPS-1171, account, site manager 6, mid)

**Voices:** both; members carry 12 of 15. Both at Nordic Fitness Group and Voima.
**Stopped or continuing:** continuing. No release claims a fix; the 9 July dunning preview predates all 15. None of the 8 reviews got a reply.
**For triage:** defect. NPS-1127, 1171, 1187; REV-2004, 2008, 2017, 2020, 2032, 2033, 2044, 2059; MF-315, 317, 326, 336. Payment at the desk does not stop them; REV-2017 ended in a cancellation.

## Freeze ends early or charges

> Member asked to pause from the end of the month and I told him it would run to the first. It did not. He noticed and I had no answer. (MF-327, end user, mid)

**Voices:** both, at Nordic Fitness Group, Aktiv Hälsa and Fitness Nørrebro.

**For triage:** defect. NPS-1131, 1132, 1149, 1179, 1190, 1195; MF-327, 328, 329, 330, 339; REV-2027. The desk reports all describe a month-end pause.
