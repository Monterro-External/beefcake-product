# Denmark readiness

One Danish customer, no local payment method, and an invoice layout their
accountant rebuilds every quarter. Denmark is currently a market we sell into
without being in it.

## The decision, stated honestly

Two questions, and they have different answers:

**A. Do we fix the Danish invoice?** Yes, regardless. We have a Danish customer
and their accountant has asked every quarter for two years. This is small, it is
required by Danish practice rather than by preference, and doing it does not
commit us to the market.

**B. Do we build MobilePay and sell into Denmark properly?** Undecided. This is
the market entry question and it is the one the gate has to answer.

Keeping them separate is the point of this file. They have been discussed as one
thing for a year, which is why neither has happened.

## Evidence

[Denmark market scan](../../discovery/market/denmark-market-scan.md). Eleven
qualified Danish conversations in a year, two lost explicitly on MobilePay, one
customer.

## Appetite

- Invoice work: two weeks, unconditional.
- MobilePay and market entry: not set. The gate sets it if it says yes, and the
  scan recommends sequencing it behind Vipps because the work is adjacent and
  doing Denmark first would cost Norway.

## Kill criteria for B

Set before it starts, per principle 7: if within twelve months of MobilePay
shipping we do not have four Danish customers, we stop selling into Denmark,
support the ones we have, and say so publicly. That is a real number and a real
date and it should feel uncomfortable.

## If the answer is no

Then we say it out loud, stop taking Danish inbound, and tell Fitness Nørrebro
what that means for them. The worst outcome is the current one: a market we
neither serve nor leave.
