# Norway payments: Vipps

Norway is 27% of ARR, our second market, and the payment method every Norwegian
adult uses is not one we support.

## Why

Vipps appears in every Norwegian sales call and cost us two deals this year. It
is raised at the desk weekly at our second-largest account. Three independent
sources: sales, CSM notes and the request channel.

The operational argument is stronger than the popularity one. Norwegian sign-up
happens at the desk with a phone in hand. AvtaleGiro needs a bank login and takes
days to activate, so the choice today is a card payment now and a mandate later,
or a member who leaves and thinks about it. Kraft Trening are counting how many
sign-ups start and are not completed; that number will be the honest evidence.

## What is in scope

- Vipps for the first payment at sign-up.
- Vipps for recurring, if the recurring product is available to us on terms that
  do not need a separate commercial agreement per operator.

If only the first is possible, the first is still worth doing: it is the moment
where we lose the member.

## Not in scope

- MobilePay. Same family of work, different market, different decision.
- Replacing AvtaleGiro. Members with a working mandate keep it.

## Appetite

Six weeks. If the integration turns out to need per-operator commercial
agreements with Vipps, that changes the shape of the work and it comes back to
the gate rather than expanding quietly.

## Kill criteria

- If Kraft's abandoned sign-up count comes in low, the operational argument
  fails and this drops behind the rest of the payments programme.
- If the terms require us to hold a commercial relationship per operator, stop
  and re-decide.

## Depends on

Nothing. This is deliberately independent of the corporate memberships decision
so that a slip there does not take Norway with it.
