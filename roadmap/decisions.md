# Decision log

Every entry names who decided, what was chosen, what was rejected and why, and
what would change it. A decision that is not here can be reopened by anyone. A
decision that is here is reopened only with new evidence.

---

## 2026-08-18 · Reporting goes ahead of corporate memberships

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, at the roadmap review, with Elin Sandberg and the
three PMs present.

**Decision.** The legacy report engine replacement takes the team that becomes
free in Q4. Corporate memberships stays in discovery until its December decision
date.

**Rejected.** Starting to build corporate memberships now, on the argument that
the renewal is in January. Rejected because we do not yet know which of the two
shapes we are building, and starting without that answer is how a quarter is
spent building the Swedish shape and losing the Finnish deals anyway.

**Cost accepted.** If Nordic Fitness Group leaves over corporate memberships,
this decision is why. Written down for that reason.

**Would change it.** Discovery concluding early with one clear shape, or the
renewal moving.

## 2026-08-11 · The report engine is worth a quarter

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson at the prioritisation gate. Door: two-way.
Appetite: one team, one quarter.

**Decision.** Fund the replacement, hold it to KR2.3, and treat it as
engineering-led with product supplying the observable requirements only.

**Rejected.** Optimising the two worst reports and moving on, which is what the
ticket queue would suggest. Rejected because the queue is not evidence here: both
affected accounts work around it instead of reporting it, so ticket volume
understates it by design.

**Would change it.** Timings showing the problem is confined to one report.

## 2026-07-28 · Vipps before MobilePay, and both behind an evidence bar

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson at the gate, with Camilla Roos arguing for both at
once.

**Decision.** Norway first. Denmark sequenced behind it and treated as a market
entry test with a kill criterion. Vipps starts when Kraft Trening's abandoned
sign-up count arrives.

**Rejected.** Doing both in one piece of work. It is tempting because the work is
adjacent, and it doubles the size of a bet before the first one has returned any
evidence.

**Also rejected.** Starting Vipps on the strength of "everyone asks for it".
Everyone asking is a real signal and it does not tell us what it costs us. The
abandoned sign-up count does.

**Would change it.** A Norwegian key account making it a renewal condition, which
would move it from an evidence question to a retention question.

## 2026-06-30 · Payments reliability is a standing programme, not a project

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson.

**Decision.** Two teams, for the year, on one surface from charge to abandoned,
held to KR1.1 and KR1.2 rather than to a delivery list.

**Rejected.** A payments project with a scope and an end date. Rejected because

## 2026-05-19 · No family or household memberships

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, after the third quarter in which it was the most
frequent feature request in the support queue.

**Decision.** No. Reviewed annually, or when the evidence changes.

**Reasoning.** High volume, concentrated in the long tail, low ARR impact, and
structurally the same data-model change as corporate memberships without the
strategic argument. If corporate memberships is built, this is re-examined,
because the second one is cheap if the first exists.

**Rejected.** Building it because it is asked for most often. Frequency of
request is not the same as value, and this is the clearest example we have.

**Would change it.** Evidence that it wins or keeps mid-segment operators, rather
than that single-site owners ask for it. Nobody has produced that evidence in
three years of asking.

## 2026-04-14 · The member surface stays minimal

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, prompted by two proposals to expand the member app.

**Decision.** The member app does booking, subscription self-service within what
the operator allows, receipts and access. Nothing else without a CPO decision.

**Reasoning.** The vision says members never think about the software. Every
feature we add to the member surface is a reason for them to.

**Consequence, acknowledged.** This blocks one branch of the churn tree: letting
a member fix a failed payment themselves. That branch may be worth the exception
and it needs to be argued as an exception rather than slipped in.

## 2026-03-03 · No custom development for a single account, including key accounts

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, after a request from a key account for per-site
invoice numbering.

**Decision.** A need becomes a tenant setting available to everyone, or it is
declined in writing by the CPO with the reasoning.

**Precedent.** The per-site invoice numbering built for Nordic Fitness Group in
2024, still behind an undocumented flag, and implicated in two receipt defects
since.
