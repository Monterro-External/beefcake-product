# Decision log

Every entry names who decided, what was chosen, what was rejected and why, and
what would change it. A decision that is not here can be reopened by anyone. A
decision that is here is reopened only with new evidence.

---
## 2026-09-22 · The Q4 team goes to the report engine

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson at the prioritisation gate. Door: two-way.
Appetite: one team, one quarter.

**Decision.** The team that becomes free in Q4 replaces the legacy report engine,
held to KR2.3 and engineering-led, with product supplying the observable
requirements only. Corporate memberships stays in discovery until its December
decision date.

**Rejected.** Starting corporate memberships now on the strength of the renewal
calendar. Rejected because the shape is unresolved: the Swedish block purchase
and the Finnish part-payment are different requests, and a quarter started
without that answer is a quarter spent building the Swedish shape and losing the
Finnish deals anyway. Rejected also is the ranking our own RICE produced on the
first pass, 0.56 against 0.40 in corporate memberships' favour, which counted two
key accounts as a reach of two. Weighted by share of the book the ranking
inverts, and it stays inverted after removing the account that appears on both
sides.

**Cost accepted.** If Nordic Fitness Group leaves over corporate memberships,
this decision is why. They are simultaneously the renewal this defers and one of
the two accounts whose reports do not complete, so the decision is a bet that
their real risk is the accumulation of small failures rather than the one missing
capability.

**Would change it.** Discovery concluding early with one clear shape, or the
renewal moving, or timings showing the report problem is confined to a single
report, which would free the team before the quarter ends.

**Relation to 2026-08-18.** The same call, re-decided at the gate rather than at
the roadmap review. That entry stands as the record of when the order was set;
this one records the arithmetic behind it, which the earlier entry did not have:
reach as share of the book, and the inversion surviving the removal of the
account counted on both sides. This is the entry to reopen.

## 2026-08-25 · Corporate memberships: a first slice, Finnish shape first

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson at the prioritisation gate. Door: one-way.
Appetite: one quarter of one team.

**Decision.** Fund a first slice of corporate memberships: an employer as a
payer, an employee list, one invoice, and the Finnish shape (employer pays a
share of one membership) built before the Swedish block purchase. The wider
initiative keeps its decision date of 2026-12-15.

**Rejected.** Building the Swedish block first, which is what our largest account
described and what the pre-mortem's top failure story warns about. The red team's
recommendation was adopted: the Finnish shape is harder, it generalises, and the
block falls out of it more easily than the reverse.

**Also decided.** We take our percentage on the whole membership value including
the employer's share. The pre-mortem surfaced this as a commercial question with
no owner, which is how it would have been answered by whoever wrote the invoicing.

**Cost accepted.** A quarter of a team, on a one-way door, on the thinnest
evidence base of anything currently funded. The red team said so plainly and the
argument is recorded rather than resolved.

**Would change it.** SC-1 failing: if an operator cannot set up an employer
without us, it does not ship.

**Consequence.** The family memberships decision of 2026-05-19 said it would be
re-examined if corporate memberships was built. That re-examination is now open
as BC-0019.

## 2026-07-28 · Vipps before MobilePay, and both behind an evidence bar

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson at the gate, with Camilla Roos arguing for both at
once.

**Decision.** Norway first. Denmark sequenced behind it and treated as a market
entry test with a kill criterion. Vipps starts when Kraft Trening's abandoned
sign-up count arrives.

**Rejected.** Doing both in one piece of work. It is tempting because the work is
adjacent, and it doubles the size of a bet before the first one has returned any
evidence.

**Also rejected.** Starting Vipps on the strength of "everyone asks for it".
Everyone asking is a real signal and it does not tell us what it costs us. The
abandoned sign-up count does.

**Would change it.** A Norwegian key account making it a renewal condition, which
would move it from an evidence question to a retention question.

## 2026-05-19 · No family or household memberships

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, after the third quarter in which it was the most
frequent feature request in the support queue.

**Decision.** No. Reviewed annually, or when the evidence changes.

**Reasoning.** High volume, concentrated in the long tail, low ARR impact, and
structurally the same data-model change as corporate memberships without the
strategic argument. If corporate memberships is built, this is re-examined,
because the second one is cheap if the first exists.

**Rejected.** Building it because it is asked for most often. Frequency of
request is not the same as value, and this is the clearest example we have.

**Would change it.** Evidence that it wins or keeps mid-segment operators, rather
than that single-site owners ask for it. Nobody has produced that evidence in
three years of asking.

## 2026-04-14 · The member surface stays minimal

**Decided by:** Malin Ohlsson, prompted by two proposals to expand the member app.

**Decision.** The member app does booking, subscription self-service within what
the operator allows, receipts and access. Nothing else without a CPO decision.

**Reasoning.** The vision says members never think about the software. Every
feature we add to the member surface is a reason for them to.

**Consequence, acknowledged.** This blocks one branch of the churn tree: letting
a member fix a failed payment themselves. That branch may be worth the exception
and it needs to be argued as an exception rather than slipped in.
