## Intent

A parent can pay for a membership held by someone under 18.

## Problem and evidence

Filed as its own request. It is the same underlying need as BC-0004 seen from a different angle: a membership whose payer is not the member.

> Kan en förälder betala för ett barns medlemskap? I dag krävs barnets egna bankuppgifter och en fjortonåring har inga. SUP-10183, 2026-05-05.

## Next steps

Merge with BC-0004. Note for whoever does the merge: this framing, payer is not the member, is the same shape as corporate memberships and may be answered by that work.
