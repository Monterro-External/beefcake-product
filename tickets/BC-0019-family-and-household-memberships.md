## Intent

A household pays once for several memberships.

## Problem and evidence

Answered with a no on 2026-05-19 and reopened deliberately, because the decision
that closed it said it would be: *"If corporate memberships is built, this is
re-examined, because the second one is cheap if the first exists."* The corporate
memberships first slice passed the gate on 2026-08-25.

The demand has not changed and has never been in doubt. It is the most frequent
feature request in the support queue, it is concentrated in the long tail, and it
has been consistent for at least three years.

> "Har ni familjemedlemskap? En mamma och två tonåringar vill ha en betalning och
> tre kort." SUP-10141, 2026-04-15. Three more in the same quarter.

What has never been produced, in three years of asking, is evidence that it wins
or keeps a mid-segment operator. That absence is the reason for the no, and it is
still the question.

## Discovery state

This is a re-examination with a narrow brief, not an open discovery. Two
questions and nothing else:

1. **Is a household membership the same object as an employer-paid one?** Both
   are a membership whose payer is not the member. If corporate memberships makes
   this nearly free, the economics change.
2. **Does it move anything in the mid segment?** Long-tail volume is not the
   test. It has never been the test.

## Next steps

1. Wait for the corporate memberships shape to settle. Until then question 1
   cannot be answered and this ticket should not consume anyone's week.
2. Ask ten mid-segment operators whether they would use it and what they do
   today. Owner: Aino Lehtinen.
3. If the answer to both questions is no, close it again and record the reason so
   the fourth year of asking has something to point at.
