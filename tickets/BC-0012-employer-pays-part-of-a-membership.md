## Intent

An employer pays a share of one membership and the member pays the rest.

## Problem and evidence

Finnish operator. This is a different shape from an employer buying a block of memberships, and the difference matters.

> Employers here often pay part of a membership as a benefit. We want the employer to pay a share and the member to pay the rest, on the same membership. SUP-10297, 2026-06-24.

## Next steps

Attach to the corporate memberships discovery. Do not merge with the Swedish block-purchase requests: the two shapes are being kept separate deliberately.
