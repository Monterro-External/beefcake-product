## Intent

A member with a failed payment receives a notification from the app every day.

## Problem and evidence

One operator, one member. Triaged as a member app notification defect. Front desk could not stop the messages from their side.

> Member came in with her phone to show us the messages. One a day since the run. She had paid at the counter on the second day. Asked if she should cancel the card to make them stop. SUP-10256, 2026-06-08.

## Next steps

Ask the app team whether notification frequency is configurable. Low priority: one member at one tenant.
