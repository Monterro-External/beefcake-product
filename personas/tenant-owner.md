# Owner, multi-site operator

**Kristin, 47.** Owns Vestkyst Trening: three sites around Bergen and Stavanger,
1,700 members, eleven employees. Bought the second site in 2021 and the third in
2024, and has not yet decided whether there will be a fourth.

Segment: mid. ICP fit: best. Representative of about 80 operators.

Last updated 2026-02-18 after the payment-experience round. Evidence:
[interview 2025-11-13](../discovery/interviews/2025-11-13-owner-vestkyst-trening.md).
Two round B conversations (2026-01-20, 2026-02-03) are transcribed in
`../discovery/interviews/transcripts/` and not written up; what they add is
provisional until they are.

## What she is actually responsible for

Revenue this month against revenue last month, the payroll that goes out
regardless, and the rent on three leases. Members are a number that has to move
in the right direction. She has no operations manager, so she is also the person
who covers a shift when someone is ill.

## Goals

- **Know the number without asking anyone.** Members in, members out, revenue,
  and whether the trend is her doing or the season's.
- **Not lose members for reasons that are not about the gym.** A member who
  leaves because they moved city is a cost of doing business. A member who leaves
  because a payment failed and nobody told her is a loss she could have prevented.
- **Grow without adding administration.** The fourth site has to be run by the
  same office staff as the third.
- **Not be the vendor's test case.** She has been through one system migration
  and will not do another voluntarily.

## Frustrations

- Reports that require her to know which report to run. She wants the answer,
  and gets a menu.
- Finding out about a problem from a member. Twice in the last year the first
  she heard of a billing issue was a member showing her a phone screen at the
  front desk.
- Numbers that disagree between two screens. Once that happens she stops
  trusting all of them, and it takes a quarter to recover.
- Being sold a feature when she asked about a defect.

## How she uses Beefcake

Ten minutes on a Monday morning, on a laptop, in the office behind the reception
at the Bergen site. Revenue and churn for the month, then the failed-payments
list if the payment run was recent. She exports to CSV and works in a
spreadsheet, because that is where her accountant lives. She has never opened
the class schedule.

She has an owner login at all three sites and has never used the site filter.

## What she says

> "I do not want a dashboard. I want to know if something is wrong, and if
> nothing is wrong I want to close the laptop."

> "The month I found out from a member instead of from you, that is the month I
> started asking what else I do not know."

## What this means for us

- Reporting is a retention feature for this persona, and its failure mode is
  silence rather than slowness.
- Anything that goes wrong with money needs a path that reaches the owner before
  it reaches a member. She will forgive the defect and not the surprise.
- Export to CSV is not a nice-to-have here. It is how the persona's real workflow
  ends.
