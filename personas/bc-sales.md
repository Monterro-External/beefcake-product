# Sales (Beefcake)

**Jonas Solberg.** Account executive for Norway, one of six in sales under
Camilla Roos. Carries new logos and key-account expansion in his market. Three
years at Beefcake, previously sold field-service software.

## What the job actually is

Twelve to twenty conversations a week with operators who are unhappy with
something, usually their current vendor's billing. Demos, migration questions,
and a pipeline where the deciding question is almost always what happens to the
customer's existing payment mandates.

## Goals

- **Not lose on a missing checkbox.** He loses deals in Norway to Vipps and he
  has said so in every pipeline review this year.
- **Know what is coming and when**, precisely enough to say it to a prospect
  without creating a commitment product has not made.
- **Have the reference call be easy.** His best asset is an existing customer in
  the same city who will take the call.

## Frustrations

- Roadmap answers he cannot repeat to a customer.
- Finding out at the demo that something behaves differently in his market.
- The gap between "we are looking at it" and a date, which he is asked to fill
  personally in front of the prospect.
- Losing to Boxmaster on corporate memberships in a deal he had otherwise won,
  which has happened twice this year.

## What he brings product

Lost-deal debriefs, demo transcripts, and the questions prospects ask that never
become support tickets because the person asking is not yet a customer. This is
the only signal source that hears from the market rather than from the book of
business, and it is the earliest warning we get.

## The tension worth naming

Sales is measured on this quarter and product is measured on the year. The
prioritisation gate exists so that a deal-specific ask has to clear the same bar
as everything else, and product principle 6 exists so that the conversation ends.
Both make Jonas's job harder in the short run, and he agrees with them in
pipeline reviews and disagrees with them in deals.
