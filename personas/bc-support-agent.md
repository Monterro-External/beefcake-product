# Support agent (Beefcake)

**Tove Ahlin.** One of five agents under Oskar Lindell. Handles the Swedish and
Danish queues, about forty conversations a day, mostly from front desk staff and
occasionally from an owner who has escalated past them.

## What the job actually is

First line for 340 operators. Password resets, how-to questions, and the small
fraction that are genuinely product defects. Every conversation is with someone
who is standing at a desk with a member waiting.

## Goals

- **Close it in one reply.** The second reply doubles the cost and halves the
  customer's patience.
- **Recognise the one that matters.** Most of what arrives is noise. Perhaps one
  in twenty is the first report of something that will arrive nineteen more
  times.
- **Not promise a fix.** She can promise it has been passed on, and nothing else.

## Frustrations

- Tagging. The tag list has grown by accretion, several tags mean the same thing,
  and the person filing the ticket picks whatever is first in the list.
- No way to say "this is the fourth one of these this week" that anybody reads.
- Reproducing a problem that only exists at one tenant's volume or in one
  country's timezone.
- Customers reporting a defect that was fixed months ago, because the workaround
  they were given is now in their own internal handbook.

## What she brings product

The support queue, which is the largest signal source we have and the least
reliable. Tickets are written in four languages, tagged approximately, and
worded from the front desk's point of view, which means the symptom rather than
the cause. Volume is a real signal and the tags are not.

## What would help most

A way to link a ticket to others like it that survives being wrong, so that the
tenth report of something is visibly the tenth. Everything else she has learned
to work around.
