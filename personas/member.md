# Member

**Hanna, 34.** Trains three or four times a week at Puls Träning Kista, mostly
classes, mostly at 18:00. Has been a member for two years and pays 549 SEK a
month by Autogiro. Has our app on her phone and uses it to book classes.

Not our customer. The operator's customer, and the person who feels every defect
first.

Last updated 2026-05-18. Evidence: three round B member conversations
(2026-04-13, 2026-04-15, 2026-04-27), recorded and transcribed in
`../discovery/interviews/2026-04-round-b-payment-problems/transcripts/` and not yet written up. By the rule in
`README.md` every claim below is provisional until they are.

## What she wants from the software

Almost nothing. A class booked, a door that opens, and the amount she expects
leaving her account on the day she expects it. She has no loyalty to the software
and no idea who makes it.

## Goals

- **Get into the class she wants**, including when it says full, which is why the
  waitlist matters to her more than anything else we build.
- **Not think about the membership.** She set it up once and does not want a
  relationship with it.
- **Not be embarrassed at the door.** A card that does not work in front of
  other people is disproportionately upsetting.

## Frustrations

- Messages about money. A failed payment produces a sequence of notifications
  that she reads as increasingly severe, and none of them tells her whether the
  problem is at her end or the gym's.
- Not knowing where she is on a waitlist, or finding out she was promoted after
  the class started.
- Freezing over the summer and coming back to a surprise. She has frozen twice
  and checked her bank statement carefully both times.
- Anything that requires phoning the gym during opening hours.

## How she behaves when something goes wrong

She does not contact us. She does not know we exist. She tells the front desk,
or she posts a one-star review naming the gym, or she stops paying and lets the
membership lapse rather than cancelling. The third is the one that costs the
operator the most and generates no signal at all except in the data.

## What she says

> "I got a message saying the payment failed, then another one, and I could not
> tell if I was supposed to do something. I had not changed anything."

> "I asked the girl at the desk and she said it was the system. Fine, but what am
> I supposed to do?"

## What this means for us

- Member-facing communication during dunning is a product surface with a
  measurable outcome, and today it is a series of notifications nobody designed
  as a sequence.
- The absence of a support ticket is not the absence of a problem. Member pain
  arrives as churn data, app-store reviews and front-desk anecdote, and it
  arrives late.
- Self-service that removes a phone call to the gym is worth more to this
  persona than any feature we could add to the app.
