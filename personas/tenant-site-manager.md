# Site manager

**Jarno, 33.** Runs the Tampere Keskusta site of Voima Kuntosalit: one of five
sites, about 700 members on his floor, nine staff on a rolling rota, thirty-two
classes a week. Reports to a regional manager who reports to the owner.

Segment: mid and key account. ICP fit: best. Representative of roughly 400 people
across our book.

Last updated 2026-02-18. Evidence:
[interview 2025-11-18](../discovery/interviews/2025-11-18-site-manager-voima-kuntosalit.md),
[interview 2025-11-06](../discovery/interviews/2025-11-06-site-manager-puls-traning.md).

## What he is actually responsible for

The schedule being full, the instructors turning up, the members being happy
enough not to phone the regional manager, and the door working. He is measured
on class fill rate and on member complaints, and he can influence the first
directly.

## Goals

- **A full schedule with no empty slots and no angry waitlist.** Fill rate is his
  number and he watches it weekly.
- **Know before the members do.** If a class is cancelled, if the door is
  playing up, if a payment run has gone badly, he wants twenty minutes of
  warning so he can be in front of it.
- **Spend his time on people.** Every minute in an admin screen is a minute not
  on the floor, and he is the person members recognise.

## Frustrations

- The waitlist. He does not fully trust it, so he checks the roster by eye before
  popular classes, which is a job the product was supposed to remove.
- Discovering a member's complaint is actually a system problem after he has
  already apologised for something that was not his fault.
- Reporting that only aggregates across sites when his conversation with his
  regional manager is about his site.
- Changes that appear without warning. He is the person who has to explain them
  to nine staff.

## How he uses Beefcake

Constantly, in short bursts, on a laptop at the desk and on a phone on the floor.
Schedule and roster daily, member lookup several times a day, the access log when
somebody says their card did not work, fill rate weekly. He does not touch
payments and does not have the rights to.

## What he says

> "I check the list before the 18:00 class anyway. It takes two minutes and it
> has saved me twice, so now I always do it."

> "When a member says the app is broken, I have no way of knowing whether the app
> is broken."

## What this means for us

- A feature the site manager does not trust is a feature that costs him time
  instead of saving it. Trust is recovered by being visibly right, and the
  waitlist is the current example.
- The gap between something going wrong and him knowing about it is the number
  to optimise for this persona.
- He needs per-site views everywhere, because his accountability is per site even
  when the tenant is not.
