# Customer success manager (Beefcake)

**Marte Bakken.** CSM for the Norwegian book and one of the two key accounts,
one of four CSMs under Henrik Dahl. Five years at Beefcake, two of them in
support, which is why she reads error messages the way an engineer does.

## What the job actually is

Owning tenant health for roughly eighty operators: weekly contact with the key
accounts, monthly with the mid segment, and a health score that sorts the rest.
Renewals, escalations, expansion, and being the person an owner phones when they
have decided to be angry.

## Goals

- **No surprise churn.** A renewal that fails should have been visible in the
  health score a quarter earlier.
- **Turn an account's pain into a signal product can act on**, rather than into
  a promise she has to keep herself.
- **Keep the escalation path short.** When a key account escalates, the value is
  in the first four hours.

## Frustrations

- Reporting a pattern and being asked for evidence she has already sent, in a
  different format.
- Being the last to know about a release that changes what she told a customer
  last week.
- Health scores that move for reasons she cannot explain to the account.
- Watching the same defect arrive from three accounts over two months and having
  no way to show that they are the same defect.

## What she brings product

Call notes, written weekly for key accounts and monthly for the mid segment, in
her own shorthand. They are the richest signal source we have about what an
account is thinking, and the least structured. She also sees churn risk before
the data does, because she hears the tone change.

## The tension worth naming

A CSM's instinct is to fix the account in front of her, and the way to do that
fastest is a promise. Every promise made outside the gate is a commitment
product did not make and will be measured against anyway.
