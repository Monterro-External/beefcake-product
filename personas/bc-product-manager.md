# Product manager (Beefcake)

**Johan Wirén.** PM for Growth and payments, one of three PMs. Owns pillar 1 and
the two teams working on charges, dunning, mandates and receipts. Four years at
Beefcake, previously in payments at a PSP.

The other two: Ingrid Halvorsen (Operations and access, based in Oslo, owns
pillar 3) and Aino Lehtinen (Member experience, based in Helsinki).

## What the job actually is

Turning a noisy stream of signals into a small number of tickets that engineering
can build without a second discovery phase, and defending the ones that were not
chosen. Roughly a third of the week is signals and discovery, a third is
authoring and grilling tickets, and a third is the conversations that keep a
decision from being reopened.

## Goals

- **Find the pattern before the tenth report.** Most of the 34-day median in
  KR3.1 is time spent before anyone connected several reports to each other.
- **Hand over tickets that come back as questions rather than as rework.**
- **Say no with a reason that survives being repeated by somebody else.** Sales
  and CS repeat the reason more often than they repeat the decision.

## Frustrations

- Signals arriving in eight places with no shared identifier: support, NPS, CSM
  notes, sales calls, Slack, app reviews, the platform exports, and a colleague
  stopping him in the corridor.
- Being asked for a date before the problem is understood.
- Discovering that a decision he documented was relitigated in a meeting he was
  not in.
- Evidence that turns out to be one loud customer wearing three hats.

## How the work runs

Signals to triage to discovery to the gate to a ticket to handover, as described
in `../CLAUDE.md`. The Product Ticket is the artifact he owns. He does not write
the Spec and does not put implementation into a ticket, which is a habit that
took a quarter to break.

## What good looks like

A ticket where every claim carries a link, the non-goals are specific enough to
be annoying, and the regression scenario names the thing he would be most upset
to see broken.
