# Releases

Release notes as they go out to operators, plus the internal version CS reads
first. One file per release. Written by the PM who owned the largest item, sent
by CS on the morning of the release.

Rules that exist because we have broken them:

- **CS sees it two working days before the customer.** They are the ones who
  will be asked about it.
- **Say what changes for the person using it**, not what was built.
- **Known issues are in the notes.** A customer who finds out from us is annoyed.
  A customer who finds out from a member is looking at competitors.
- No version numbers in customer-facing notes. Operators do not have versions,
  they have a product that changed on a Thursday.
