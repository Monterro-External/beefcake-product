# What changed on 14 May

## Class schedule

**Week numbers in the schedule are correct again.** The header of the weekly view
showed the previous week's number for some date ranges. It now matches the
calendar in all four markets, including the turn of the year, which is where it
went wrong in the first place.

If your staff have a note somewhere telling them to ignore the week number,
this is the release where you can throw it away.

**Copying a week to another week.** You can now copy a whole week of classes to
a future week and adjust from there, instead of creating each class. Most useful
when you rebuild the schedule for a season.

## Payments

**The failed-payments list shows the next attempt date.** Where a charge is going
to be retried, the list and the member's payment history now show when. Your
front desk can answer the question they get asked most often after the payment
run without contacting us.

**Failure reasons are written in words.** The list showed the code we receive
from the bank or the payment provider. It now shows what the code means and, where
there is one, what can be done about it. The code is still there, at the end of
the line, because several of you sort and filter on it.

## Members

**Search finds people by phone number.** Members give the desk a phone number
more often than they spell their surname.

## Reports

**CSV exports open directly in Excel** in Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish
locale settings, without the import wizard.

## Known issues in this release

- Copying a week does not copy instructor assignments where the instructor is not
  available in the target week. Those classes are created without an instructor
  and appear in the schedule with a warning.
- The next-attempt date is not shown in the member app. Members still have to ask
  the desk. This is deliberate for now.

## Coming next

told us are the source of the most frequent misunderstanding at the desk.
