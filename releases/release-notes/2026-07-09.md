# What changed on 9 July

A smaller release than usual. Most of the team has been working on the payment
run itself, which is the sort of work that shows up as nothing happening.

## Payments

**Members without a valid mandate are visible before the run, not after it.**
The payments screen has a section listing members whose direct debit
authorisation is missing, expired or cancelled by their bank. Previously these
appeared as failures after the 25th, by which point the month was lost.

For most operators this list is longer on the first look than expected. That is
not new, it was simply not visible before.

**Receipts open faster** for operators with large member bases.

## Access

**The access log shows why entry was refused**, in words, next to each denied
entry. Your staff can tell a member whether the problem is their membership,
their card or the door.

## Settings

**The dunning settings page shows the schedule it produces.** When you change the
number of attempts or the days between them, the page lists the dates a charge
failing today would be retried on. Several of you have told us the settings are
hard to reason about; this is the first step and there is more to do.

## Known issues in this release

- The mandate list does not distinguish between a member who never set up a
  mandate and one whose bank cancelled it. Both appear as missing. We know which
  is which and are not showing it yet.
- The dunning preview shows the schedule for a charge failing today. It does not
  show what will happen to charges already part way through their retries.

## A note on the summer

The payment run on 25 July and 25 August goes out as normal. Support is at
reduced staffing between 13 July and 3 August, with the same cover for anything
affecting payments or access.
