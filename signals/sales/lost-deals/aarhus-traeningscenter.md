# Lost deal: Aarhus Træningscenter

Two sites in Aarhus, about 1,400 members. Inbound, they found us. Lost at the
second demo, which is the fastest I have lost anything this year.

## Why we lost, in their words

> "MobilePay er ikke til forhandling. Og vores revisor skal kunne bruge
> fakturaerne uden at bygge dem om."

MobilePay is not negotiable, and their accountant has to be able to use the
invoices without rebuilding them.

## What actually happened

Question one in the first demo was MobilePay. I said card only and that Denmark
is a market we are investing in, which was as far as I could honestly go. We
kept the meeting going but I could feel it end there.

The invoice point came second and was more specific than I expected. Danish
receipts have to show the VAT rate and amount, and invoice numbers have to run
consecutively per year. Our only Danish customer works around this by rebuilding
the export every quarter. When I could not say we do it properly, the owner said,
not unkindly, that we did not seem to sell to Denmark.

## What we did well

Nothing that mattered. They liked the member app.

## What would have won it

MobilePay and a Danish invoice layout. Both are table stakes rather than
advantages: having them would have got us into the race.

## Honest note

This is the third Danish conversation this year that ended on the same two
questions. Either we do Denmark properly or I would rather we told sales to stop
taking Danish inbound, because it is a bad experience for them and for us.
