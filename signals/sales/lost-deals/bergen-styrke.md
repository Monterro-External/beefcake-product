# Lost deal: Bergen Styrke

Four sites around Bergen, about 2,100 members. In the pipeline since
January. Two demos, one on-site visit, a proposal at
2026-03-29.

## Why we lost, in their words

> "Vi kan ikke selge et medlemskap hvor folk ikke kan betale med Vipps. Det er
> ikke en funksjon for oss, det er sånn folk betaler."

Translated: we cannot sell a membership people cannot pay for with Vipps. It is
not a feature to them, it is how people pay.

## What actually happened

Vipps came up in the first demo and I gave the honest answer, which is that we
support AvtaleGiro and card and that Vipps is not scheduled. They kept talking
to us for six weeks after that, which I read as the question being survivable.
It was not.

The second factor, which I think mattered more than they said: their sign-up
happens at the desk, on a phone, in about two minutes. AvtaleGiro takes days to
become active and needs a bank login. Gymly let them take the first payment
immediately with Vipps and set up the recurring mandate afterwards. That is a
concrete operational difference on the busiest day of their year, and I could
not match it.

## What we did well

Reporting and multi-site access impressed them. Their operations lead said our
class and waitlist handling was better than what they bought. We were not
outsold on the product overall.

## What would have won it

A Norwegian payment method their members recognise, and the ability to take the
first payment at sign-up. In that order.

## Honest note

I have logged Vipps in the requests channel five times this year. I do not need
a date. I need to know whether the answer is no, because if it is no then I
should stop working four-site Norwegian gyms and spend the time elsewhere.
