# Lost deal: Kuntokeskus Espoo

Three sites in the Helsinki area, about 2,900 members. Lost at proposal.

## Why we lost, in their words

> "Työnantajat maksavat osan jäsenyydestä. Meidän täytyy laskuttaa työnantajaa
> suoraan, ei jäsentä."

Employers pay part of the membership. They need to invoice the employer
directly, not the member.

## What actually happened

Employer-paid memberships are more common in Finland than anywhere else we sell.
Not the whole membership: a share, with the employee paying the rest, on one
membership. It is a tax-advantaged benefit here and it is normal.

Our answer is two memberships per person, which their operations manager
correctly described as something that would confuse both members and their own
reporting. Our existing Finnish customer does exactly that and dislikes it, which
I did not volunteer.

## What we did well

Access hardware, five-site experience, and the multi-site class schedule. Their
site managers liked the product. As at Stockholm Strength Collective, the people
who would use it daily preferred us.

## What would have won it

A split payer on one membership: employer pays a share, member pays the rest,
one card, one receipt each.

## Honest note

Corporate memberships is being discussed as a Swedish key-account request. In
Finland it is a market-entry requirement and it looks different: it is a split of
a single membership, not a company buying a block. If we build only the second we
will not win deals like this one.
