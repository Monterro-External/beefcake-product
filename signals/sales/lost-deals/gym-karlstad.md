# Lost deal: Gym Karlstad

Single site, about 640 members. Lost on price to Gymly.

## Why we lost, in their words

> "Ni är 40 procent dyrare och jag ser inte vad jag får för det. Jag har ett gym,
> inte en kedja."

We are 40% more expensive and he cannot see what he gets for it. He has one gym,
not a chain.

## What actually happened

Straightforward. Gymly quoted lower, he asked us to match, we did not. He was
polite and he was right about his own business: most of what we are better at is
multi-site, and he has one site.

He asked about a family membership twice, which is the only feature question he
raised. Gymly do not have it either, so it did not decide anything.

## What we did well

Nothing to fix. The product was not the issue.

## What would have won it

A lower price, which we should not offer. This prospect is outside the ICP: one
site, 640 members, no multi-site need, and a support cost that would eat the
margin at the price he wanted.

## Honest note

Logging this as a loss is arguably wrong. We should count it as a
disqualification and stop working single-site prospects under about 800 members
in markets where Gymly is active. That is four losses this year on the same
shape.
