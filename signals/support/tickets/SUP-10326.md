# Waitlist promotion does not free the class the member was holding

**Riikka Salo** (owner) at Kuntoklubi Kallio · email · 2026-09-19 11:06

I have reproduced this three times, so I am fairly confident in the steps.

Setup: a member is booked on class A and waitlisted for class B, where A and B overlap. That is normal here, people hold a spot on the earlier session while they wait for the one they want.

Steps:
1. Someone cancels out of class B, so a place opens.
2. The promotion runs.
3. The member is promoted into B and gets the email.
4. The member is still booked on A.

Expected: the booking on A is released, because the member cannot be in two places and the system knows it.
Actual: both bookings stay. A shows as full while the member is in B.

Effect at our end: the 17:30 sessions look full on the screen and there are four or five empty places in the room. My instructors have started keeping a paper list. Yesterday we turned away two people from a class that had space.

Members: I can give you three member numbers if you want them, but you can find it yourself by looking for anyone with two overlapping bookings. There should not be any.

Riikka
